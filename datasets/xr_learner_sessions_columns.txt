DATASET: XR Learning Assistant — Synthetic Learner Sessions
What this file is:
  This is a plain-English guide to the columns in xr_learner_sessions.csv.

What one row means:
  One row = one learning session for one learner using an XR learning system.

Important notes:
  • The dataset is synthetic (simulated), but the numbers were generated to behave in realistic ways.
  • Some columns can be blank (missing) when the value simply doesn’t exist yet:
      - days_since_prev_session is blank for a learner’s very first session.
      - prev_quiz_mean and prev_quiz_last are blank until a learner has taken at least one quiz.
  • “Ratio” columns run from 0 to 1:
      - 0 means “none of the time” and 1 means “all of the time”.

Targets (things you try to predict):
  • next_quiz_score (0–100): the learner’s quiz score after the session
  • time_to_mastery_days: how many days until they reach “mastery” (here: quiz score ≥ 85)
  • support_required (0/1): whether the learner needs extra support next session

COLUMNS (name | plain meaning | typical values):

session_id | A unique ID for this session (like a receipt number). | e.g., S_0000123
learner_id | A unique ID for the learner (so you can group their sessions). | e.g., L_0042
session_index | Which session number this is for that learner (1st, 2nd, 3rd…). | 1, 2, 3…
session_start_iso | When the session started (date and time). | YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS
day_of_week | The day of the week for the session start time. | Mon, Tue, …
time_of_day | A simple time bucket for when the session happened. | Morning/Afternoon/Evening/Night
days_since_prev_session | How many days since this learner’s previous session (blank for their first session). | 0.0, 2.5, 10.0…
device_type | What kind of device/platform was used for XR. | VR_HMD, AR_Tablet, Desktop_3D
input_mode | How the learner mainly controlled the system. | Controllers, HandTracking, Gaze, Voice, Mixed
accessibility_nonvoice_enabled | Whether a non‑voice accessibility option was enabled (1=yes, 0=no). | 0 or 1
avg_latency_ms | Average system ‘lag’ during the session (higher = more delay). | 10–220 ms
tracking_loss_ratio | How often tracking glitched/was lost (0 = never, higher = more often). | 0.0000–0.2500
session_duration_min | Total length of the session in minutes. | 4–90
attention_ratio | How focused the learner was (share of time on‑task). | 0.05–0.98
active_time_ratio | How much of the session they were actively doing things (not idle/paused). | 0.05–0.98
pause_count | How many pauses/breaks happened. | 0–20
help_request_count | How many times the learner asked for help/hints. | 0–15
hint_time_sec | Total time spent looking at hints (in seconds). | 0–1800
error_count | How many mistakes/incorrect attempts happened in practice tasks. | 0–40
completion_rate | How much of the planned session content they finished. | 0.02–0.99
video_count | Number of video items used in the session. | 0–18
reading_count | Number of reading items used in the session. | 0–22
audio_count | Number of audio items used in the session. | 0–12
practice_count | Number of interactive practice activities attempted. | 0–45
modality_switches | How many times they switched between video/reading/audio/practice. | 0–50
dominant_modality | Which content type they used the most (or Mixed if no clear winner). | Video/Reading/Audio/Practice/Mixed
prev_quiz_mean | Average score across earlier quizzes (blank if no earlier quizzes). | 0–100 or blank
prev_quiz_last | Score on the most recent earlier quiz (blank if none). | 0–100 or blank
prev_quiz_attempts | How many quizzes the learner had taken before this session. | 0, 1, 2…
prior_mastery_level | System’s estimate of how much they knew before the session (0=low, 1=high). | 0.0000–1.0000
confidence_rating | Confidence rating (1=low confidence, 5=high confidence). | 1–5
next_quiz_score | TARGET: quiz score after this session (what you predict in regression). | 0–100
time_to_mastery_days | TARGET: estimated days until the learner reaches mastery (quiz ≥ 85). | 0–120
support_required | TARGET: whether the learner needs extra support next session (1=yes, 0=no). | 0 or 1
split | Which data split this row belongs to (assigned per learner to avoid leakage). | train / val / test
